## Word additions (all §2-verified, [wiktionary] cited)

- **freuen** (row 615) — froh + -en, PG *frawaz 'quick, glad'; English relative is **frolic**
  (NOT free — verified trap).
- **fühlen** (616) — PWG *fōlijan, exact twin of English feel.
- **anziehen** (617) — an + ziehen, separable; sich anziehen = get dressed.
- **wenn** (618) — PWG *hwan, twin of when, doublet of wann.
- **ob** (619) — PG *jabai 'when, if', true cognate of English **if**.

## Etymology notes verified during authoring

- "Speak, hands, for me!" — Casca, *Julius Caesar* Act 3 Scene 1 (verified).
- konnte↔could, sollte↔should, wollte↔would, musste↔must, durfte↔archaic *durst*,
  wusste↔archaic *wist* — preterite-present pasts frozen into English presents.
- wäre↔were (PG *wes-), hätte's -te ↔ -ed dental suffix (OE hæfde).
- Strong/weak adjective declension ↔ OE strong gōd mann vs weak se gōda mann.
- sich ↔ OE *sik reflexives; "hie thee hence", "get thee to a nunnery", "help yourself".
- Relative der/die/das ↔ OE þe/that; whom ↔ wen/wem case endings.
- als ← OHG alsō = al + sō, twin of English **also** (not a *hwan word) — used in 5111.
- Vater↔father: NOT V→B (Verner's law) — respected; the concurrent session's 5091 handles
  it correctly.
