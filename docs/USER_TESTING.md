# First-run user test kit

The goal is to find out where a real beginner loses the thread, not to prove the
app works. Run this with 5 people who have not seen Brücke.

## Setup

- Use a fresh browser profile or private window.
- Give the tester the URL only. Do not explain the product first.
- Ask them to think aloud. Do not rescue them unless they are stuck for 60 seconds.
- Record the screen and note timestamps; do not prompt during the first minute.

## Tasks

1. **Landing:** “What do you think this site is for?” Then let them choose how to start.
2. **First lesson:** Ask them to finish the first lesson without help. Note whether
   they read the hook, pattern, table and practice, and where they scroll or pause.
3. **Completion:** Ask them to leave the lesson and return home. Did completion
   persist? Could they find Review?
4. **Review:** Ask them to start a review session and grade one card. Note whether
   the grade buttons were visible without hunting.
5. **Share/resume:** Send them a lesson URL (including a non-German one), then ask
   what language/course they think they are in.
6. **Mobile pass:** Repeat tasks 1–3 at 390×844.

## What to record

- Time to first lesson and time to finish lesson 1.
- Every point where they say “what do I do?” or click a dead end.
- Whether they notice the language of the lesson and the language of the UI.
- Whether they return voluntarily the next day.
- One quote that explains their mental model of the product.

## What not to do

- Do not ask “do you like it?” Ask them to do something and observe.
- Do not add analytics or accounts before the first five sessions are reviewed.
- Do not turn a single tester’s preference into a product rule; look for repeated
  breakdowns across at least three people.
