# Brücke — Executive Summary

*Compiled 2026-10-08 · v0.1*

> **You already understand more German than you think.**
> Brücke teaches English speakers German by decoding the 1,500-year-old sound-shift rules (500–800 AD) the two languages share — turning memorization into deduction.

## The problem
Language apps optimize for streaks, not fluency. Learners grind thousands of disconnected flashcards and still can't read a sentence. Meanwhile, trust in AI-generated course content is collapsing (Duolingo's 2025 "AI-first" backlash), and the category leader's stock fell ~70% in a year while its learners complain the app "doesn't work."

## The insight
English and German are siblings split by one historical event: the Second High German Consonant Shift (500–800 AD). Learn ~9 sound-shift rules (7 consonant laws + strong-verb Ablaut + the -ieren pattern) — `water→Wasser`, `make→machen`, `think→denken`, `two→zwei` — and hundreds of German words become *derivable* from English instead of memorizable. Every lesson is an "aha" moment of connection, never a vocab list.

## The product (built and shipping)

**Four pillars:**
1. **The Trail** — 168 authored lessons across 30 topic clusters on a game-style trail map with star-gate mastery locks
2. **The Sound Shift Atlas** — 9 shift constellations, radial explorer with philological sources
3. **The Review Hub** — SM-2 spaced repetition, 5 decks × 4 review styles, anti-spoiler design
4. **Production drills** (inspired by Language Transfer's celebrated methodology): learners produce German from thought — transcription, literal-gloss, and twist exercises live today, with the full Thinking-Method cue-ladder rollout in progress

**Behind them:**
- **A 1,226-word sourced etymological compendium** — every derivation tagged with its source (Wiktionary/etymonline; 8 entries flagged UNVERIFIED pending review); invented connections are a fireable offense
- 234 automated tests, WCAG-audited design system, 187 themes, 43 fonts — craft quality aimed at the Monkeytype/polyglot community
- **100% static, local-first, account-free**: zero telemetry, zero marginal infra cost, full data export

## Why now
1. AI-content backlash makes "human-crafted & verifiable" a moat, not a cost.
2. Duolingo's stumbles leave a quality-seeking segment underserved.
3. The architecture (static, multi-language-ready) scales to Dutch, Swedish, Spanish, French — each language gets its own sound-change/etymology mapping authored on the shared engine, so each new language multiplies content leverage.
