# Brücke — Funding Roadmap (Phase 0 → Series A)

*Compiled 2026-10-08 · v0.1*

*Assumes: solo technical founder, working product, no revenue, no legal entity yet.*
*Reference point: Fluent Forever — $4.9M seed @ $13.5M valuation on a method + community; the language-learning category was actively funded in 2026 (Lucida AI $7M seed, July 2026).*

---

## Where you are (start of roadmap)

**Assets:** complete, tested, documented product; a genuinely differentiated pedagogy; a built-in distribution niche (Monkeytype/typing + polyglot communities); architecture with ~zero marginal cost.

**Gaps (canonical register — `01` §6 points here):**
1. No users measured → no retention/cohort metrics yet (by design; analytics is Phase 0's first fix — investors need numbers).
2. No revenue, no legal entity, no pricing experiments yet.
3. No backend → no cross-device sync, no accounts, no payments yet.
4. Single founder; no mobile-native app (PWA packaging is a funded Phase 4 item).
5. Curriculum depth: 656 unique taught words — Goethe-A1 coverage (650 words) complete; A2/B1 expansion needed.
6. No formal efficacy evidence yet (pre/post tests, cohort study — funded as 10% of this round).
7. Monkeytype theme/font licenses not yet cleared for headline marketing; no "Brücke" trademark search yet (Phase 0).

Investors at this stage buy *proof of pull* — so the roadmap's first job is manufacturing proof, in cheap order.

---

## Phase 0 — Make it fundable (Weeks 1–4, ~$500 — runs in parallel with Phase 1)

1. **Incorporate** (Delaware C-Corp for US VCs, or GmbH/ Ltd depending on target market; 83(b)/vesting sorted).
2. **Add opt-in, privacy-preserving analytics** (PostHog self-hosted or Cloudflare Web Analytics): activation, lesson completion, D1/D7/D30 retention, SRS adherence. This is the single highest-leverage change — it converts "built app" into "measurable product."
3. **Fix the legal gray area & brand risk:** Language Transfer's Thinking Method is cited as inspiration — confirm the license/credit posture, and keep the history of derived content clean. Clear the Monkeytype theme/font licenses before using them as a headline differentiator, and run a "Brücke" trademark clearance.
4. **Landing page + waitlist** at brucke.app (domain acquisition: confirm availability and budget — not yet owned) with a 60-second landing demo loop of the "water→Wasser" moment — distinct from the 3-minute investor cut (see outreach kit).
5. **Advisors:** recruit 1–2 names learners trust (a linguistics PhD; a language-learning YouTuber). Advisory equity 0.25–1%, vesting.
6. **German legal baseline before any German traffic:** Impressum (TMG §5) and DSGVO privacy policy on the landing page; cookie-consent tooling for PostHog; minors data / AVV (data-processing agreement) review before any B2B school pilot.

## Phase 1 — Proof of pull (Months 1–3, running in parallel with Phase 0)

Goal: real users, retention curve, and a community — before any investor meeting.

- **Crowdbeta launch:** r/languagelearning, r/German, r/Monkeytype (theme/font culture is your organic wedge), Hacker News ("Show HN: I taught an app to think in sound shifts"), X/Polyglot Twitter, Language Learning Discord.
- **Creator seeding:** send keycaps-worthy demo clips to 10–20 language-learning and mechanical-keyboard creators.
- **Kill-criteria / pivot triggers written down** (investors respect this): e.g., if D7 < 15% after 1,000 registered learners, the onboarding is wrong, not the method.
- **Targets to exit Phase 1:** 1,000+ waitlist, 500+ weekly active learners, D7 ≥ 25%, 20+ pieces of unprompted user content, NPS > 40.
- Cost: ~$2–5K (hosting, tooling, small creator gifts). Fundable from personal funds the whole way.

## Phase 3 — Pre-seed round (Months 5–6)

- **Ask: $350K on a SAFEs.** Range check vs. 2026 market: pre-seeds land $150K–$1M (Carta/Antler); a product-complete, metrics-having, community-backed app justifies the upper-middle. Post-money valuation target ~$3–5M — anchored to comparable seed valuations, not revenue multiples (a pre-revenue app has no meaningful revenue multiple): Fluent Forever's $13.5M seed is the documented ceiling; Carta's pre-seed medians sit well below.
- **Terms:** standard non-participating 1x; no liquidation shenanigans; keep board control.
- **Target investors:** the full roster and deal-shape table (accelerators, thesis-fit edtech funds, angels, syndicates) lives in `05-investor-outreach.md` — dig for warm intros on OpenVC/Crunchbase/Signal.
- **Round construction:** $100K accelerator (if accepted) + $150K angels + $100K problem-VC or founder-friendly micro-fund — sums to the $350K ask. Close in ≤6 weeks with momentum. (A YC acceptance changes the math: take the ~$500K standard deal and upsize, or skip the angel tranche.)

## Phase 4 — Use of funds & proof points (Months 6–18)

**Allocation of the $350K:**
| Bucket | % | What |

**Proof points that unlock the seed round:**
| Metric | Target by Month 18 |
|---|---|

| D30 retention | > 20% (top-quartile for education apps) |

| Efficacy | A published cohort study showing measurable A1 progress |
| Languages live | 3 |

## Phase 5 — Seed & beyond (Month 18+)

- **Seed raise $1.5–3M** on the above metrics (comp: Fluent Forever $4.9M; Lucida AI $7M in 2026).
- **Strategic optionality:** partnership or licensing conversations with Goethe-Institut-adjacent publishers, typing-culture brands (Monkeytype outreach — collab themes only after license clearance), keyboard makers.
- **Series A gates:** multi-language flywheel working, content production system demonstrably scalable (authors + AI-assisted drafts with the verification protocol), $1M ARR.

## Risk register (be ready to answer these)

| Investor question | Your honest answer |
|---|---|
| "Solo founder?" | Hiring plan funded by round; 2 content leads are the first two hires. |
| "Duolingo crushes you?" | They can't copy trust; their cost base (AI infra, growth) is yours to skip pre-sync — sync, payments, and creator seeding are your only real costs. |
| "Method works?" | Language Transfer's method has over a decade of organic adoption; we add measurable production drills + SRS — and we'll publish the study. |
| "Why not AI-generated content?" | AI is a drafting tool inside a verification protocol; trust is the brand. |
| "What if nobody pays?" | Pricing experiments start Month 6; $8–12/mo sits in the category's standard band (Babbel, ELSA, Preply pricing pages — re-verify before each meeting); zero infra cost means profitability at low absolute revenue. |
| "Why German first?" | Highest English-cognate correlation of any major language = the strongest possible demo of the method; 15–20M learners; employment migration into DACH. |
